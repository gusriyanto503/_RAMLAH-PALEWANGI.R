Ekspresi & Kalimat Praktis

Simple Present Tense & frasa untuk mendeskripsikan rutinitas

📐 Simple Present Tense untuk Rutinitas
Gunakan tense ini untuk menyatakan kebiasaan dan rutinitas sehari-hari
📌 Rumus Dasar

Untuk kalimat positif tentang rutinitas:

Subject + Verb (s/es) + Object/Complement

⚠️ Ingat: Tambahkan -s atau -es pada kata kerja jika subjeknya He, She, It.

✅ I wake up early. → Tanpa -s (I = orang pertama)
✅ She wakes up early. → Tambah -s (She = orang ketiga)
✅ He goes to school by bus. → go + es = goes
❓ Kalimat Tanya (Question)
Do/Does + Subject + Verb (bentuk dasar) + ?
Do you wake up early? → Kamu bangun lebih awal?
Does she go to school by bus? → Apakah dia pergi ke sekolah naik bus?
What time do you have breakfast? → Jam berapa kamu sarapan?
🚫 Kalimat Negatif (Negative)
Subject + do not (don't) / does not (doesn't) + Verb + ...
I don't watch TV in the morning.
She doesn't eat breakfast at school.
He doesn't go to bed late.
💬 Adverb of Frequency

→

🌟 Frasa Berguna untuk Bercerita
First, I wake up and pray. (Pertama, aku bangun dan berdoa.)
Then, I take a shower and get dressed. (Lalu, aku mandi dan berpakaian.)
After that, I have breakfast with my family. (Setelah itu, aku sarapan bersama keluarga.)
Next, I go to school. (Berikutnya, aku pergi ke sekolah.)
Finally, I go to bed at 9 PM. (Akhirnya, aku tidur pukul 9 malam.)

Rangkuman Materi

Poin-poin penting yang telah kamu pelajari dalam modul ini

🌅 Morning Vocabulary

  • Wake up — Bangun tidur
  • Pray — Berdoa / Sholat
  • Brush teeth — Sikat gigi
  • Take a shower — Mandi
  • Get dressed — Berpakaian
  • Have breakfast — Sarapan
  • Go to school — Pergi ke sekolah

☀️ Daytime Vocabulary

  • Attend class — Menghadiri kelas
  • Study — Belajar
  • Take notes — Mencatat
  • Have lunch — Makan siang
  • Do homework — Mengerjakan PR
  • Play with friends — Bermain
  • Go home — Pulang

🌙 Evening/Night Vocabulary

  • Exercise — Berolahraga
  • Cook dinner — Memasak makan malam
  • Have dinner — Makan malam
  • Watch TV — Menonton TV
  • Review lessons — Mengulang pelajaran
  • Take a bath — Mandi malam
  • Go to bed / Sleep — Tidur

📐 Simple Present Tense

  • Positif: S + V(s/es) + O
  • Negatif: S + don't/doesn't + V
  • Tanya: Do/Does + S + V + ?
  • He/She/It → tambah -s atau -es
  • I/You/We/They → tidak berubah
  • Go → Goes; Watch → Watches

⏰ Time Expressions

  • in the morning / afternoon / evening
  • at night / at + jam
  • every day / every morning
  • before / after + aktivitas
  • first, then, after that, finally
  • always, usually, often, sometimes, never

💬 Frasa Penghubung

  • First... — Pertama...
  • Then... — Kemudian / Lalu...
  • After that... — Setelah itu...
  • Next... — Berikutnya...
  • Finally... — Akhirnya...
  • Before / After — Sebelum / Sesudah
🏆 Contoh Paragraf Lengkap

My name is Rani. I wake up at 5 AM every day. First, I pray and then I take a shower. After that, I get dressed in my school uniform. I always have breakfast with my family before I go to school. At school, I attend class and take notes. I have lunch at noon. After school, I go home and do my homework. In the evening, my mother cooks dinner and we have dinner together. Before I go to bed, I always review my lessons. Finally, I sleep at 9 PM.
